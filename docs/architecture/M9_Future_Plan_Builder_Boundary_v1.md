Not implemented. Builder may later take level, domain/goal, event date, weeks, sessions/week, equipment, days, constraints.

It may assemble **only** from:

- published session-template versions
- authored progression structures
- compatible placements
- explicit prerequisites

It must not pull arbitrary fragments without programme-level authored progression and validation. Assembled plans are **authored-assembly**, not “AI-generated” unless a separate product decision says so.
