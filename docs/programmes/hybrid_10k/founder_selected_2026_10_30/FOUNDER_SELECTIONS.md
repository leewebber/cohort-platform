> D1 — Monday chest press
> Bilateral incline dumbbell press, approximately 30° bench.
> 3 × 6–10, 2 RIR, 2 minutes rest. Log external load per dumbbell.
>
> D2 — Thursday calf raise
> Bilateral standing calf raise on a machine.
> 2 × 10–15, 2 RIR, 60–90 seconds rest.
> Do not substitute unilateral dumbbell calf raises.
>
> D3 — Core placement and setup
> Monday: standing square-stance cable Pallof press, cable at chest height,
> both hands on the handle, press without torso rotation.
> 2 × 10 per side, controlled execution, 45–60 seconds rest.
>
> Thursday: bodyweight side plank.
> 2 × 30–45 seconds per side, 45–60 seconds rest.
>
> These replace the attachment's conflicting placement/reverse crunch.
> Do not convert RIR into a requirement to reach failure on core work.
>
> D4 — Thursday reverse lunge
> Two dumbbells at the sides, neutral grip.
> Complete one side then the other.
> 1 × 6 per leg, 2–3 RIR, 90 seconds rest after both sides.
> Log external load per dumbbell.
>
> D5 — Monday pull-up
> Strict shoulder-width overhand pull-up.
> 3 × 5–8, 2 RIR, 2 minutes rest.
> Bodyweight initially; added load permitted when appropriate and supported
> by the exact retained identity.
> Assisted variants remain a separate unresolved branch, not silently
> deleted, substituted or represented using negative added load.
>
> D6 — Thursday shoulder press
> Seated with near-upright back support, two dumbbells, neutral grip,
> strict execution without leg drive.
> 3 × 6–10, 2 RIR, 2 minutes rest. Load per dumbbell.
> This supersedes the proposed standing press.
>
> D7 — Rows
> Monday: bilateral chest-supported dumbbell row.
> 3 × 8–12, 1–2 RIR, 90 seconds rest.
>
> Thursday: single-arm dumbbell row with free hand supported on a bench.
> 3 × 8–12 per side, 1–2 RIR, 90 seconds rest after both sides.
>
> Retain Monday's dumbbell lateral raise:
> 3 × 12–20, 1–2 RIR, 60 seconds rest.
>
> These are standard-week doses. Preserve the previously retained proposed
> consolidation/taper reductions visibly in the final review. Where applying
> those reductions creates ambiguity, identify it explicitly rather than
> inventing a material coaching decision.
>
> Week 10 Thursday's already-selected exception remains:
> two comfortable sets each of the selected seated press and bench-supported
> single-arm row, at least 3 RIR; no lower-body lifting.
>
