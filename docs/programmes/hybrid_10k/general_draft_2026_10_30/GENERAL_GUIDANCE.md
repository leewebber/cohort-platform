# General Hybrid 10K source guidance

Scope: general ten-week 10K PB programme for an already-fit, competent runner.
The retained early doses and all unselected detailed prescriptions remain draft;
this reconciliation does not approve the whole programme or its entry workload.
Lee's return-to-running assumptions, travel dates, treadmill-to-Korea transition,
travel-reduced Sunday and future personal HYROX race objective are excluded from
this general candidate. They remain intact in the historical Lee source.

The weekly template is Monday strength A, Tuesday main run, Wednesday easy run,
Thursday strength B, Friday easy/strides, Saturday rest, Sunday long run or final
test. Week 10 Thursday is retained upper-body work, not rest: two comfortable sets
each of the retained DB shoulder press and bench-supported one-arm DB row, at least
3 RIR, no lower-body lifting. Rep ranges and rests remain from their retained
prescriptions; no new upper RIR bound or gym duration estimate is invented.

## Controlling targets

Confirmed Zone 2 is primary on main EASY running work. The marker is the existing
`athlete_confirmed_zone_2`. Athlete confirmation, exact source review/acceptance and
the canonical immutable occurrence snapshot are required. Missing configuration
refuses start; supporting RPE cues do not silently substitute a target. Warm-up,
cooldown and recovery instructions do not acquire invented numerical HR limits.

Quality-labelled threshold and current-10K work use existing
`alpha_quality_pace_v1` categories `threshold` and `10k_specific`. The unchanged
[approved rules](../../../checkpoints/m23_quality_target_admission/DECISIONS.md)
control `alpha_5k_8w_riegel_1_06_v1`, `alpha_manual_threshold_pm2_v1`, and
`authored_rpe_v1`: freshness, matching civil timezone, original formulas/bands,
rounding, manual-threshold-only selection and exact source/input/review freeze.
The earlier prohibition on a 5K-derived threshold reference and earlier automatic
pre/post-week-4 pace regimes are superseded. A representative 5K is explicitly
entered/confirmed/selected under those rules; there is no in-programme 5K test and
no automatic ingestion or fabricated baseline.

Supporting authored effort cues remain: EASY RPE 3–4, RECOVERY RPE 2–3, STEADY
RPE 5–6, THRESHOLD initially 7/up to 8 at the end, TEN_K about 7–8 early/at most 9
at the end, STRIDE about 7–8 relaxed. They do not authorize competing pace rules.
The source contains no selected integer `fallback_rpe` for marked quality work.
No range is collapsed into a scalar. The existing authored-RPE alternative stays
unavailable unless its exact required scalar values are explicitly authored and
then explicitly chosen/reviewed; no automatic fallback is introduced.

The four founder-selected replacement runs have a 13-minute warm-up (10 easy +
3×[20-second relaxed acceleration + 40-second very easy running]), explicit
between-only recoveries and a 5-minute very easy running-or-walking cooldown.
No fixed target is prescribed for those accelerations/recoveries. The exact
replacement body ends at that cooldown; no extra mandatory walk is inserted.
Programmed duration includes the cooldown. Actual walking is excluded from
running-volume calculations, which retain a five-minute bound for each choice.

## Retained shared run guidance — draft, subordinate to exact replacements


For EVERY run: 3–5 minutes brisk walking beforehand and 3–5 minutes walking afterwards, additional to prescribed RUNNING duration. Add 10 ankle rocks/side, 10 calf raises, 10 controlled leg swings/side before starting. Easy runs start with five especially gentle minutes INCLUDED in their prescribed duration. No stretching holds during hard running.

Quality sessions include their own easy-jog warm-up/cooldown in the session steps. After warm-up, optionally pause for 2–3 minutes of familiar dynamic drills, then start repetitions; do not add unprescribed fast running. Run recoveries at RECOVERY effort. Prescribed interval counts and between-only recoveries are exact. All easy-jog warm-ups/cooldowns count as running volume; walking and drills do not.

Strides are INCLUDED in the listed total run duration: easy lead-in, then N × (20 seconds relaxed quick run + 100 seconds very easy jogging). Thus the full stride block is N × 2 minutes, including recovery after the last stride. Do not add another stride block beyond the listed time. On a treadmill, use controlled acceleration and a safe speed; if transitions are awkward, execute the entire duration easy and record the omission.


For the four selected replacements the exact warm-up/cooldown above supersede any
conflicting inherited preparation sequence. For other retained runs, extra walks
and untimed drills are guidance outside the programmed running subtotal, not
fabricated timed phases. Exact body totals do not include an unknown transition.

## Retained strength guidance — detailed content still draft


RIR = repetitions remaining with good technique. Choose actual loads from current ability; no guessed kg prescriptions. Bodyweight pull-ups are valid; add weight only if the target reps and RIR are met. If needed use foot-assisted pull-ups with a secure setup (or an available assisted-pull-up machine in Korea). Record assistance separately from added external load.

Standard tempo: controlled lowering (~2 seconds), no forced pause, purposeful lift. Calf raises: ~2 seconds up, 1-second top pause, ~2 seconds down; full comfortable range. Lateral raises: controlled both ways. Carries: normal walking, tall posture, no running. Per-side repetitions/durations apply to EACH side; left+right form one set. RIR does not apply to holds/carries: stop before posture/grip breaks.

Warm-up for each gym session: 5 minutes brisk walking/marching, 10 bodyweight squats, 8 unloaded hinges, 8 scapular pull-ups or shoulder-blade movements, 8 arm circles each direction. For the first loaded lower-body exercise add 2–4 progressively loaded warm-up sets; for each new upper-body pattern add 1–2 light sets as needed. No warm-up set to failure. Finish with 3–5 minutes easy walking.

Repetition ranges are intentional: record completed reps. Once ALL work sets reach the top of the range at target RIR on two exposures, athlete may accept the smallest available load increase (typically 1–2 kg per DB / 2–2.5 kg upper-body barbell / 2.5–5 kg lower-body barbell), returning to the bottom of the range. Never force a load jump or automatically progress. Maintain if running quality or recovery is affected. No 1RM tests or failure training in this block.

If Monday lunges impair Tuesday running, the explicit athlete-selected adjustment is omit Monday lunges for that week; do not move extra leg volume elsewhere. If Thursday strength impairs Sunday long runs, first remove one squat work set. Shoulder pressing uses comfortable range and neutral DB grip if preferred; omit a painful movement and flag it rather than prescribing through pain.


Week 10 Thursday has upper-body preparation only. No squat/hinge or loaded lower-
body work is inserted by the shared preparation template. Warm-up work is separate
from the four comfortable prescribed upper-body work sets.

## Progression, fatigue and taper boundaries

Preserve the authored phase/week structure, repeat counts, work/recovery order,
consolidation weeks and reduced lower-body work. Do not derive later progression
from logged results. The original generic fatigue/pain guidance remains proposed:
stop/shorten affected work for focal or worsening pain/altered gait; an athlete may
review a 20–30% easy/long reduction or equal-duration easy variant for accumulated
fatigue. These are source proposals; M3 adaptation admission remains open. Nothing
here automatically changes or reschedules a prepared or future workout.

If running increases are not tolerated, review the last tolerated structure and
remaining schedule rather than compressing missed training. No new readiness test,
progression percentage or source-specific general entry standard is invented.

## Final assessment and logging

The sole distance-ended workout is the final Sunday measured 10K. Its retained
warm-up, strides/final recoveries, settle jog, exact 10,000 m work and cooldown
remain distinct. Use actual elapsed time, not moving time, and keep the measured
segment separate from the session's warm-up/cooldown. Record environment/route,
splits, RPE and available HR as actuals; missing results stay unavailable. No
Cohort-to-benchmark ingestion or comparison eligibility is implied.

Strength records keep exercise and set identity, actual load with per-dumbbell vs
barbell interpretation, per-side reps/time, achieved effort/assistance and notes
where supported. The source never prepopulates actuals. Separate typed assistance,
per-side achieved RIR and all requested endurance observation fields are not yet
proved for this source.
