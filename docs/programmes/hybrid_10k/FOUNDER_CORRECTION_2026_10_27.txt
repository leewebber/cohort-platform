Correction to the Hybrid 10K brief:

The previously suggested week-one and week-two running durations are
withdrawn and must not be treated as approved.

The programme is a ten-week 10K PB plan for an already-fit, competent
runner. It should have substantial aerobic work and progressive quality
training, with four runs and two strength sessions per week.

Easy runs are guided by the athlete's configured Zone 2 heart rate.
Quality-interval pace windows are calculated from a 5K benchmark.
The entry standard, exact durations, individual workouts and pace
calculation rules remain to be specified and approved.

Lee's personal return-to-running circumstances may require a separate
adjustment for his alpha use; do not silently change the general
programme or assume that adjustment has been designed.

Continue the M1.1 representation and execution assessment. Do not invent
or publish prescriptions. Keep Garmin delivery and activity return
