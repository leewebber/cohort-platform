FOUND​​ER BRIEF — HYBRID 10K

Programme: ten weeks, 2 November 2026 to 10 January 2027, ending with a
Sunday 10K test. Four runs, two gym sessions and one rest day each week.

Weekly structure:
- Monday: Gym A, strength maintenance and upper body.
- Tuesday: quality run; initially easy/steady, later threshold.
- Wednesday: easy run.
- Thursday: Gym B, upper-body emphasis and a small lower-body dose.
- Friday: easy at first; later controlled supporting quality.
- Saturday: rest.
- Sunday: long easy run.

Weeks 1–2 are a return to running. Training starts on a treadmill;
travel is expected around 14 November, with outdoor running thereafter.
Durations below are provisional, include warm-up and cooldown, and depend
on recovery. Long runs are included in the weekly totals.

Week 1: 100–115 min total; long run 30–35 min; four easy runs.
Week 2: 110–125 min; long 35–40 min; mostly easy, optional short steady.
Week 3: 125–145 min; long 40–45 min; introduce controlled threshold
        repetitions and relaxed strides.
Week 4: 115–135 min; long 40–45 min; consolidation and optional 5K
        benchmark if ready.
Week 5: 140–160 min; long 45–50 min; build threshold, modest faster work.
Week 6: 150–175 min; long 50–55 min; progress volume and repeatable work.
Week 7: 135–155 min; long 45–50 min; consolidation, retain short quality.
Week 8: 160–185 min; long 55–65 min; 10K-specific development.
Week 9: 145–170 min; long 45–55 min; final specific work, reduce fatigue.
Week 10: taper and brief sharpening; 10K test on Sunday.

Illustrative workouts, not final prescriptions: 3 x 5 min threshold in
weeks 3–4; 3 x 7–8 min threshold in weeks 5–7; and 4 x 1 km as an
example of 10K-specific work in weeks 8–9. Training paces should reflect
demonstrated fitness; sub-40 pace is not assumed.

Gym A: reverse lunge 2 x 5–6/leg; pull-up 3 x 5–8; DB overhead press
3 x 6–10; calf raise 2 x 10–15; ab exercise 2–3 sets.

Gym B: Romanian deadlift 2 x 5–6; DB bench or incline press 3 x 6–10;
chest-supported or one-arm row 3 x 8–12; lateral raise 2 x 12–20;
optional curls and triceps 2 sets each.

Gym sessions are intended to take 45–60 minutes. Avoid failure and keep
lower-body soreness low. Reduce leg volume in week 9 and omit demanding
lower-body lifting in test week. If Monday lunges interfere with Tuesday
running, the brief proposes reducing their dose or moving it to Thursday.
