# Approved athlete-facing Hybrid 10K wording

Founder-approved against e676e31 on 4 November 2026. This is additive explanatory content; all selected prescriptions and target rules remain unchanged. [Exact source-slot applications](ATHLETE_WORDING.json) cover ten weekly introductions, ten Tuesday sessions, ten Friday sessions and twenty strength sessions. No Wednesday/Sunday session introduction is replaced.

## 5K freshness message

Your 5K result must be no more than 56 local calendar days old when you start this workout. If you have a newer qualifying result, update your input and review the targets. Otherwise, this workout remains unavailable. This programme does not add a 5K test. Threshold workouts also support the separately selected manual-threshold option. A workout already started keeps its accepted targets.

## Session introductions

### Tuesday

This is the week’s main quality run. Follow the selected target and complete the work and recoveries exactly as written.

### Easy/stride Friday — W1–5 and W10

Today supports Tuesday’s quality work and the weekend’s run. Follow the easy prescription, adding relaxed strides only where listed.

### Short-5K Friday — W6–9

This shorter 5K-paced session complements Tuesday’s 10K-specific work. Keep its prescribed repetitions and recoveries unchanged.

### Strength — Monday and Thursday

Use this week’s exact sets, rests and repetitions in reserve. The selected consolidation, lower-body control and taper instructions manage strength workload alongside the running.

The freshness message applies to all fourteen quality sessions listed in [the requirement](FRESHNESS_REQUIREMENT.md). Its manual-threshold sentence applies only to the five threshold Tuesdays in W1–5. It does not create that alternative for 10K-specific or short-5K work.
