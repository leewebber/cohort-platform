## Four reusable preparation proposals

**These new ramp/dynamic details are proposed, not retrospective founder approval.** Existing easy starts and two-round movement doses are retained. All ramp/rehearsal sets are separate from working sets. RDL/lunge loads are selected by feel; no benchmark arithmetic.

| Day | Reusable prescription | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Gym A | 5 min easy rowing; two rounds (8 bodyweight squat; 8 glute bridge; 6 dead bug/side; 10 light band pull apart) in about 4–5 min. After the circuit, perform 2–3 short squat ramp sets: empty bar or very light × 8; add load × 5; if useful, a final heavier × 2–3. Work towards, never above, the planned first working load; each ramp feels comfortable with several reps spare. Use about 30–60 s to change load/recover, then 60–90 s before the first working set. If the working load is light, omit the unnecessary heavier ramp. Working sets still rest 150–180 s. | 12–15 min |
| Gym B | 5 min easy easy bike; two rounds (8 unloaded hip hinge; 6 reverse lunge/side; 10 light row) in about 4–5 min. Perform two RDL ramp sets: very light × 8, then a moderate load × 5, both comfortably short of fatigue; rest about 30–60 s while changing load. Choose by feel and hinge control, not a squat benchmark. Before the first press/row pair, take one brief easy rehearsal of each if needed; this is preparation, not another working pair. | 12–15 min |
| Gym C | 5 min easy ski erg; two rounds (6 reverse lunge/side; 8 light scapular row; 8 wall slide) in about 4–5 min. Perform two front-rack reverse-lunge ramp sets: light dumbbells × 4 per leg, then a moderate pair × 2 per leg, with about 30–60 s to recover/change load. Keep balance and the front rack comfortable. Choose loads by feel, never a squat benchmark. Before the first press/row pair, take one brief easy rehearsal if needed. | 12–15 min |
| Quality | 10 min easy run; 2 min dynamic circuit: 8 bodyweight squats, 6 reverse lunges/leg, 8 unloaded hinges, then easy walk/jog for any remainder. 3 × 20 s relaxed accelerations with two 40 s easy recoveries. Allow 40–160 s transitions. | 15–17 min |

Gym allocation is 5 min easy + 4–5 min circuit + 3–5 min ramps/setup = 12–15 min. These fill the existing preparation allowance; they do not add working sets. Rehearsal fits inside the ramp/setup allowance. If extra preparation is needed, extend honestly rather than rush or delete work. Approved working-set rests are unchanged. Quality preparation adds only the proposed two-minute dynamic circuit and a stated transition allowance. No rep tempo or exact full-gym duration is invented. The approved quality main/cooldown and long-run five-plus-five remain unchanged.
