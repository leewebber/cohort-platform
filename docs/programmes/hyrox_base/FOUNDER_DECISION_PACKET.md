## Approved reusable preparation

| Day | Approved prescription | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Gym A | 5 min easy rowing; two rounds of 8 bodyweight squat, 8 glute bridge, 6 dead bug/side, 10 light band pull apart. After the circuit, perform 2–3 short squat ramp sets: empty bar or very light × 8; add load × 5; if useful, a final heavier × 2–3. Work towards, never above, the planned first working load; each ramp feels comfortable with several reps spare. Use about 30–60 s to change load/recover, then 60–90 s before the first working set. If the working load is light, omit the unnecessary heavier ramp. Working sets still rest 150–180 s. | 12–15 min |
| Gym B | 5 min easy easy bike; two rounds of 8 unloaded hip hinge, 6 reverse lunge/side, 10 light row. Perform two RDL ramp sets: very light × 8, then a moderate load × 5, both comfortably short of fatigue; rest about 30–60 s while changing load. Choose by feel and hinge control, not a squat benchmark. Before the first press/row pair, take one brief easy rehearsal of each if needed; this is preparation, not another working pair. | 12–15 min |
| Gym C | 5 min easy ski erg; two rounds of 6 reverse lunge/side, 8 light scapular row, 8 wall slide. Perform two front-rack reverse-lunge ramp sets: a light barbell front rack × 4 per leg, then a moderate barbell front rack × 2 per leg, with about 30–60 s to recover/change load. Keep balance and the front rack comfortable. Choose loads by feel, never a squat benchmark. Before the first press/row pair, take one brief easy rehearsal if needed. | 12–15 min |
| Quality | 10 min easy running; two-minute circuit of 8 bodyweight squats, 6 reverse lunges/leg and 8 unloaded hinges, with easy walking/jogging for any remainder. Then 3 × 20 s relaxed accelerations with two 40 s easy recoveries; allow 40–160 s transitions. | 15–17 min |

Ramp/rehearsal sets are separate from working sets. RDL/lunge loads are selected by feel, never derived from squat benchmarks. Gym budgets are 5 + 4–5 + 3–5 minutes, with setup/rehearsal inside the ramp allowance. No rep tempo or compulsory full-session time is invented. Longer preparation is recorded honestly rather than cutting approved work. Long-run preparation/cooldown remains exactly five plus five minutes.
