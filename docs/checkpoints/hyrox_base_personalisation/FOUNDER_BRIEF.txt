PROGRAMME DECISIONS

HYROX Base is eight weeks of aerobic and basic strength development before specific HYROX preparation. Base describes purpose, not beginner status. Athletes can be developing or advanced. Minimum running ability: 45 minutes continuously. Six days/week, one session/day, generally 60–90 minutes.

Weekly order:
Mon Gym A: back squat; strict overhead press + pull-ups; suitcase carry; rowing intervals.
Tue easy run.
Wed Gym B: RDL; incline DB press + chest-supported row; long-lever plank; mixed conditioning.
Thu quality run.
Fri Gym C: front-rack reverse lunge; DB shoulder press + split-stance single-arm cable row; Pallof press; SkiErg/wall balls.
Sat long easy run.
Sun rest.

All three aerobic sessions intend running; eligible bike substitutions preserve effort/duration, not running pace. Substitutions must use verified app rules and athlete acceptance; do not invent exercise mappings.

Weeks 1–3 build, Week 4 mainly reduces gym volume/finisher demand with modest running reduction, Weeks 5–7 build, Week 8 consolidates. No compulsory post-Base assessment, but athletes need representative benchmarks before the next plan.

PERSONALISATION RULES V1

Run:
10 km pace = completion time / 10, in seconds/km.
Time-based controlled intervals use:
4–5 min repetitions: pace × 1.00–1.05.
6–7 min: pace × 1.02–1.07.
10 min: pace × 1.03–1.08.
These are authored Base-specific starting windows, not measured physiological thresholds. Approximate RPE 7, repeatable efforts.
Easy/long running uses conversational effort/RPE about 3–4, with established HR guidance if available; do not manufacture HR zones.

Row:
2 km split = completion time / 4, seconds/500 m.
2 min repetitions: +10–15 sec.
3 min: +12–18 sec.
Week 4: +18–23 sec.
Week 8: +15–20 sec.
These apply to dedicated rowing intervals, not rowing inside mixed WODs.

Back squat:
W1 3x8, 80% of 5RM, 3 RIR.
W2 3x8, 82.5%, 2–3 RIR.
W3 4x6, 87.5%, 2 RIR.
W4 2x6, 70%, 4 RIR.
W5 4x6, 87.5%, 3 RIR.
W6 4x5, 90%, 2–3 RIR.
W7 4x4, 95%, 2 RIR.
W8 2x5, 75%, 3–4 RIR.

Round loads to the configured available total-barbell increment, ties down. Do not guess equipment increments. RIR/technique govern athlete adjustments. Record prescribed and actual loads separately.
Other strength uses authored sets/reps/RIR and previous relevant performance; no inferred squat-to-lunge/RDL conversions.
