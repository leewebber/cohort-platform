## Principles

1. **Every athlete screen answers one question.**
   - Home: *What do I do today?*
   - Plans: *What am I following?*
   - Progress: *Am I improving?*
   - Profile: *Who am I, and how is my account configured?*

2. **Complexity belongs in the engine. Simplicity belongs in the athlete interface.**

3. **The athlete experiences coaching, not software configuration.**

4. **Information should appear where it supports the next decision.**

5. **Contextual history is more valuable than indiscriminate history.**

6. **Progress represents capability, not data volume.**

7. **Home is today, never a dashboard.**

8. **Athlete and founder experiences remain separate.**

9. **No fabricated evidence, scores, trends, or previous performance.**

10. **Athlete vocabulary is:** Plan · Today's Training · Progress · Adapt · Profile

---

## Decision checklist (future features)

Before adding athlete-facing work, answer:

1. Which athlete question does this answer?
2. Is this information needed *now*?
3. Does it belong in the athlete app or Founder Workspace?
4. Can it be shown contextually rather than as another destination?
5. Is the value supported by real evidence?
6. Does it increase visible complexity?

If answers are weak or force a fifth navigation item, defer or relocate to Founder Workspace.
