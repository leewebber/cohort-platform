# A. PROGRAMME DEFINITION

## Programme name

**Apollo Build — 12-Week Initial Block**

## Purpose

A 12-week concurrent strength, hypertrophy, athleticism and cardiovascular programme designed to:

- reduce body fat toward a maintainable **\~10–12% range**;
- retain and develop existing muscle mass;
- emphasise the visual proportions of the Apollo physique;
- build rather than sacrifice athletic capability;
- improve cardiovascular fitness and VO₂max;
- improve strength-to-weight ratio;
- maintain/improve mobility;
- prioritise posterior-chain/"Racehorse" development;
- finish in a lean, strong, energetic and maintainable condition;
- provide a launchpad into subsequent **HYROX-specific training** rather than a temporary bodybuilding/photoshoot peak.

This is explicitly **not** a bodybuilding contest-prep programme.

Week 12 should represent a condition the athlete could realistically maintain.

## Intended athlete

Experienced, well-trained male athlete with substantial existing muscle mass and concurrent strength/endurance experience.

The programme assumes competence with barbell lifting, weighted calisthenics, running, jumping and mixed-modal conditioning.

## Duration

**12 weeks**

## Phases

| PhaseWeeksPurpose               |      |                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Phase I — Foundation**        | 1–4  | Establish movements, workload, aerobic structure and progression; Week 4 reduces fatigue                                                 |
| **Phase II — Sculpt & Develop** | 5–8  | Increase training intensity, selective hypertrophy, posterior-chain stimulus, VO₂ work and athletic conditioning; Week 8 reduces fatigue |
| **Phase III — Reveal & Arrive** | 9–12 | Preserve/build priority muscle, maintain strength, sharpen running performance, finish fat loss if required and arrive fresh/athletic    |

## Equipment assumptions

Phase I must be executable with a conventional well-equipped gym.

From Phase II onward, full high-quality gym access was explicitly assumed, including:

- barbells/racks;
- pull-up/dip stations;
- dumbbells;
- cables;
- leg-curl equipment;
- back-extension station;
- trap bar;
- farmer-carry implements;
- medicine balls;
- boxes;
- sled;
- SkiErg;
- running environment/treadmill.

## Primary training priorities

**Physique:** upper chest, side/rear delts, triceps, lats, neck.

**Racehorse:** glutes, hamstrings, spinal erectors/lower back.

**Capability:** squat, hinge, pull, press, carry, run, jump and move well.

**Cardiovascular:** Zone 2 base, threshold and VO₂max.

## End-of-programme tests

Week 12 includes:

- weighted pull-up clean 3RM;
- incline bench top set of 5 @ \~1 RIR;
- RDL benchmark set of 6 @ \~1 RIR;
- max strict pull-ups;
- standing broad jump;
- 5 km time trial;
- Zone-2 pace/HR comparison;
- physique and health/recovery assessment.

# C. PROGRESSION RULES

## Resistance training

Use **double progression** inside prescribed rep ranges:

1. Maintain required RIR and technical standard.
2. Add repetitions within the range when available.
3. Once the top of the range is achieved with the prescribed RIR and technique, increase load.
4. Return toward the lower end of the rep range with the new load.

Progression is earned through:

**more load and/or more reps at equivalent technical quality and effort.**

### Never trade technique for progression

Especially:

- RDL ROM;
- strict weighted pull-ups;
- controlled compounds;
- explosive trap-bar execution.

### Failure

Compound lifts:

**No failure.**

Isolation movements:

Only where explicitly prescribed in peak weeks may the final set reach approximately **0–1 RIR**.

### If target reps cannot be completed

Do **not** add load.

Maintain or reduce load sufficiently to complete the authored rep range at the intended technical standard/RIR.

Persistent performance decline should trigger review of fatigue, nutrition, sleep and recovery rather than automatic programme escalation.

## Running progression

Progression is not merely longer duration.

Track:

**pace at comparable HR/RPE.**

For intervals:

**consistency > heroic first repetition.**

## Power work

Jumps and throws progress through:

- intent;
- quality;
- distance/velocity where measurable.

Do not progress power exercises by accumulating fatigue.

## Reduced-fatigue weeks

### Week 4

First absorption week. Volume reduced, approximately 3 RIR, controlled running and no Saturday conditioning.

Exact resistance set reductions require author confirmation.

### Week 8

Fully specified reduction to approximately **60–70% normal resistance volume**, **2–3 RIR**, reduced aerobic volume and no conditioning.

## Phase transition

Week 9 resumes productive training but does **not** rebuild Week 7's maximal hypertrophy volume.

Phase III prioritises quality/performance over continually accumulating volume.
